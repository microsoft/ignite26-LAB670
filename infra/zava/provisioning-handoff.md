# Zava pre-lab provisioning handoff — operator only

Participants start as ordinary Zava business users in a prepared environment.
Hydration, deployment, source admission and repair are provisioning tasks, not
lab exercises. Keep this runbook, generator code, private bindings, ledgers,
instructor answers and restricted-role material out of learner/agent context.

The reusable [setup toolkit](README.md) remains approval-gated. The setup team
may invoke it as a provisioning step after the exact tenant/resource/action,
permission, license and cost plan is approved. This handoff itself grants no
consent or resource authority. No live hydration is claimed complete.

## Business and naming contract

Use **Zava** as the sole named fictional company across documents, email
bodies/headers, meetings, account labels, report metadata and prompts. Use
anonymous Customer 01–03 and Supplier 01–02 identifiers for external partners,
not additional company brands. Stable account keys, schemas, model roles and
existing storage paths remain technical identifiers.

Use September 2026, USD and business-content cutoff
`2026-10-06T18:00:00Z`. Preserve later platform creation/index/refresh timestamps;
do not backdate metadata. DOC-03 version 2 is current; DOC-02 is superseded.
Bookings are not recognized revenue, graph links are not causes, copies are
not independent evidence, and the original six recovery tasks retain their
open/unassigned/undated state.

## Prepare the context estate

|---|---|---|
| Work | 105 Word documents, 128 email archives, 60 transcript archives and 581 SharePoint-ready files; current request/charter/policy and operating/commitment evidence | Full source reads under the learner identity, authority/version and duplicate-family metadata |
| People | Eight fictional colleague roles mapped privately to issued accounts or separately approved directory objects | No inference that disabled people have licensed mailboxes, sent mail or Teams history |
| Fabric data | Eleven typed managed tables, twenty model definitions with three authoritative model roles | Native table counts/hashes, schema/measures/filters and real analytical queries |
| Reports/dashboards | At least three same-named **Zava MBR** reports over distinct Finance, Commercial and archived routes; relevant Operations report and at least two dashboards where supported | Exact report IDs, workspace/owner, model lineage, audience, period, units, scenario and refresh; dashboard tiles resolved to source reports |
| Data agents / ontology | Three admitted published agents and one refreshed six-entity/five-relationship ontology | Actual query route and graph traversal under authorized identities |
| Foundry IQ | Three separate policy, operations and commercial knowledge collections with admitted client connections | Actual passage retrieval and source/version grounding; indexes/projects alone are insufficient |
| Web IQ | Issued synthetic current and older HTTPS bulletins | Full URL reads, publication time and separation of external context from internal causality |
| Business applications | Approved synthetic Salesforce opportunities, Asana tasks, ServiceNow incidents and Azure DevOps work-item/release evidence | Supported connector reads of actual fields; preserve empty assignee/date fields and explicit import/notification boundaries |

The existing generator produces model definitions, not native Power BI reports
or dashboards. The setup team must create/import those through supported,
approved authoring paths and verify their lineage separately. Similarly, agent
publication, ontology refresh, native mailbox/Teams ingestion, web hosting,
Foundry client integration and SaaS connection/import are separate admission
steps; do not call generated drafts or archives completed native services.

The expanded hero targets also require customer engagement/adoption, campaigns,
checkout measurements, release readiness and controlling launch policies.
The existing seventeen additional model views reuse core facts and do not
satisfy those data requirements. Do not silently relabel them or substitute
the recognition/expedite policy for an absent launch policy.

For Azure DevOps, admit an approved read-only connection where supported.
Otherwise the instructor may provide an explicitly labelled manual read of
authorized work-item/build/release records with exact locators and timestamps.
That is a partial rehearsal, not a tested plugin. Participants do not enable
accounts, consent to connectors, import records or deploy releases.

## Deliberate context-silo challenge

Prepare three readable reports with the same display title **Zava MBR**:

1. The current Finance report backed by the authoritative Finance model.
2. The current Commercial report backed by the authoritative Commercial model.
3. A clearly marked archive/prior-period report that is not current authority.

Expose their exact bindings in the issued source card. Retain an Operations
route for fulfillment/freight. Do not change account/period measures simply to
manufacture a bad baseline. The participant must select by task fit, lineage,
scope and freshness rather than display title.

Place current and archived policy copies in clearly labelled authorized
locations, with duplicate-family metadata linking alternate formats. Do not
create copies of private or restricted-role data to simulate this challenge.

## Learner-safe source card

Issue a permission-trimmed card with one row per admitted source:

| Field | Required value |
|---|---|
| Business role and source route | Work, Web, report/model, agent, ontology, knowledge or business connector |
| Display title and task fit | What question this source owns |
| Exact locator / report-model lineage | Actual native reference, not a fabricated URL or a logical key alone |
| Owner/authority, version and effective date | Current, historical or supporting context |
| Account/period/currency/scenario and refresh | Compatible analytical scope and distinct business/platform times |
| Duplicate family | Original versus copy/index/view |
| Permitted reader and readiness | Actual full-read/query outcome for the issued learner identity |

Do not hand participants raw `preflight.private.json`, deployment ledgers,
tokens, administrator configuration, answer keys or cross-role bindings.
Provide only their admitted references, client/build mapping, draft scope and
support path. Keep operator and learner artifacts in separate locations.

## Provisioning release gate

Generate → approve → hydrate → complete manual admission → verify learner
reads → issue the source card → rehearse baseline/curation/consumption.

Before releasing seats, independently confirm saved/reopened playbook support,
full-source reads, exact analytical routing, permissions, citations and actual
Word/app capabilities. Record each lane as ready, blocked or untested; missing
lanes cannot support an all-provider success claim. The 10–20 minute hydration
target and 75-minute learner agenda are unverified until timed rehearsals.

Use separate Microsoft-test and Skillable evidence/configuration. A Microsoft
test does not establish Skillable readiness. There is no automatic reset,
deletion, role grant, consent, paid service purchase or capacity repair.
