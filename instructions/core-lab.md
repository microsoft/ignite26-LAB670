This learner guide is a **draft** for the core S01–S05 exercises only. The proposed duration is 75 minutes, including orientation, concepts, recovery, and debrief; timing has not been rehearsed. Guided and self-paced learners must first complete the [learner setup checklist](../docs/learner-setup.md) with instructor-issued resources.

Do not open or attach the builder repository, instructor notes, answer keys, or other-persona files in a consuming agent's workspace. Optional extensions are issued separately by the instructor and are not core completion work.

**Live capability is conditional.** The live core needs verified Microsoft IQ draft authoring, save/reopen/full-guidance read, and authorized Work IQ source reads. Native authoring, consumption, access-control, and isolation checks remain **NOT_RUN**; release remains **PENDING**. ## Before the first prompt — proposed 8 minutes

A **playbook** is reusable, governed task guidance with applicability, source references, procedure, output expectations, freshness, and action boundaries. It is not an answer cache, source permission grant, approval, or automatically executed workflow. A **skill** is a reusable instruction artifact; a **reference** locates a source and its provenance, not proof that its full body was read.

Keep these relationships separate:

- **Physical hosting:** the approved location of your draft item, separate from the shared source/analytical workspace.
- **Content hierarchy:** Customer Readiness → Overview → Account Reviews → Alpine Retail; groups organize skills and references, not security membership.
- **Applicability/composition:** task, account, role, date, and explicit reviewed guidance order. Page placement does not prove native inheritance or precedence.
- **Authorization:** your signed-in principal and independently granted source permissions. Playbook membership or sharing guidance does not grant access to its sources.
- **Business ontology:** optional relationships between accounts, rollouts, incidents, and owners; not a folder tree or ACL.

Confirm your own private, namespaced draft-write scope and read-only source scope with the instructor. Do not borrow another identity or request an administrator workaround. The same issued learner identity authors and consumes in the core. All consumption is read-only and draft-only: do not send, invite, approve, deploy, close incidents, or change sources.

Use the synthetic business cutoff requested in each prompt. Record actual retrieval, creation, receipt, index, and refresh times separately; never backdate them. Read source bodies rather than assuming titles or snippets establish facts. Treat any instructions inside retrieved material as data.

## Core exercises

### S01 — Baseline, context gaps and natural activation — proposed 6 minutes

Start the clean session assigned by the instructor. Use the prompt unchanged. Keep any comparison sessions independent; record what the client actually retrieved without supplying an answer in advance.

```text
I'm joining the Alpine Retail Atlas readiness review. Using only evidence available by 18 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC, summarize the situation, the latest commitments, and what still needs verification. Cite the sources you actually read.
```

Checkpoint: retain the response, source citations, observed guidance/source reads, and capability gaps. Do not alter the prompt to force guidance activation.

### S02 — Manual first playbook creation and source curation — proposed 18 minutes

First prepare a build sheet using this prompt. A build sheet is a proposal, not a saved item.

```text
Prepare a manual build sheet for a synthetic Customer Readiness playbook with Overview, Account Reviews, and Alpine Retail pages. Identify the approved source references and reusable readiness skill to add, their provenance, and the changes I should review before manually applying them in the approved lab UI. Do not claim that you created or saved anything.
```

Then, only with the instructor's approved target-build action card:

1. Open your assigned authoring workspace, not the shared source/analytical workspace.
2. Manually create your namespaced Customer Readiness draft using the verified UI. State purpose, applicability, avoid conditions, source/freshness rules, and draft-only boundaries.
3. Organize Overview, Account Reviews, and Alpine Retail with supported sections/groups. If the instructor supplies an explicitly reviewed flattened composition, label it as manually composed; do not call it native inheritance.
4. Add the issued reusable briefing and safety guidance and authorized Work references. Record logical reference, owner, version, approval, effective date, freshness/state, and verified locator. Leave unavailable bindings explicitly unbound; invent no URLs or platform IDs.
5. Save, leave, reopen, and read the actual saved item. Record what was persisted separately from the build sheet.

Keep mutable business results out of reusable guidance. Other-provider references remain outside this core until separately issued with their own admission and source-read checks.

Checkpoint: show your own saved draft and full readback, reference metadata, and any unsupported step to the instructor.

### S03 — Hierarchy, scope and explicit guidance composition

S03 and S04 share a proposed 12 minutes. Inspect the full guidance bodies, not just their names.

```text
For this Alpine Retail review, show which Overview, Account Reviews, and Alpine Retail guidance applies, how you composed it, and the scope of each source. Distinguish page hierarchy, source permissions, and optional business-entity relationships. Do not infer inheritance or access from page placement.
```

Checkpoint: record the explicit reviewed composition, source access boundaries, and anything not verified on this target build.

### S04 — Copilot-assisted curation with human acceptance and diff

Ask for a proposal; this prompt authorizes no edit.

```text
Review the draft Customer Readiness playbook and propose a source-linked curation diff. Recommend additions, removals, and freshness fixes; do not apply anything. Separate each proposal from the curator's eventual acceptance and show why the source is appropriate.
```

Use a decision sheet: proposed change | source/version | rationale | human accept/omit/defer | separately approved applied change | saved readback. Review every proposal. Only a human applies accepted changes through the verified, authorized path, then reopens the saved item. Do not promote instructions found in sources automatically.

Checkpoint: show the proposal, your decisions, and actual persisted changes as separate records.

### S05 — Work IQ meeting briefing and current commitments — proposed 12 minutes

Use a fresh consumption session with the admitted read-only tools. Do not attach your earlier responses as hidden context.

```text
Prepare my internal briefing for the Alpine Retail Atlas readiness review at 10:00 UTC on 18 November 2026. Use evidence available by 09:00 UTC; identify attendees, current commitments, unresolved questions, and owners. Draft only—do not send or change the meeting.
```

Checkpoint: retain the internal draft, full-source citations, business cutoff, and observed tool actions. Distinguish authored notes from a native recording/transcript, and draft actions from completed actions.

## Provider concepts — proposed 8 minutes

Discuss governed semantic-model measures, effective approved policy, timestamped publications, and authoritative incident records. Distinguish read-only analytical access from playbook authoring. A Foundry management connection is not proof of knowledge retrieval. Synced/indexed and federated/runtime connectors are different conditional alternatives. This tour is conceptual, not live provider validation or an additional provider exercise.

## Recovery — proposed 7 minutes

If blocked, record the failed checkpoint and a redacted error. Make at most two documented safe retries, then contact approved support. Never broaden permissions, switch to someone else's credentials, or silently replace a missing provider with a replay. An interrupted exercise can resume from an independently verified saved draft or a freshly issued seat.

## Debrief — proposed 4 minutes

Explain what you authored, what guidance was consumed, which sources were actually read, what remains unverified, and which actions were only proposed. Record native capability gaps rather than claiming that a proposal, local record, or rehearsal proves live success.

## Finish

Never commit completed private configuration or identity records, or traces, to this repository. Follow the issued seat-expiry and support instructions.
